Schwartz-Christoffel Mapping¶

This is a collection of notes on the Schwartz-Christoffel mapping, which is a conformal mapping that transforms the upper half-plane onto the interior of a polygon. The notes cover various topics related to the Schwartz-Christoffel mapping, including its properties, applications, and examples.
